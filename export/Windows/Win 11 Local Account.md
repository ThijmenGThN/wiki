To install Windows 11 with a local account, follow the steps below:

1. Disable your internet connection.
2. Proceed with the Windows 11 installation process until you reach the "Sign in with Microsoft" screen.
3. Enter the following email address: `no@thankyou.com`.
4. Set any password of your choice, and click on the "Next" button.
5. An error message will be displayed stating that the sign-in couldn't be completed. Click on the "Next" button to proceed.
6. You can now continue setting up your local account by following the on-screen instructions.

_It is recommended to re-enable your internet connection after completing the installation process to ensure full functionality and access to relevant updates and features._
